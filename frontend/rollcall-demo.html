Entry 01 — why this exists

Spreadsheets don't know who's late.

Assignments live in shared drives, deadlines live in someone's memory, and grading feedback disappears into a chat thread. There's no single record of who was taught what, who submitted, and when — until now.

— assignments.xlsx (v14-FINAL-final2)
— "did you get my file?" (unread)
— grade sheet, three versions, three answers
— no record of the late submission that was "fine, just this once"

Entry 02 — three roles, one ledger

Everyone sees exactly what's theirs.

A

Admin

Sets up classes, subjects, teachers, and who's teaching what.

T

Teacher

Publishes assignments to their classes, reviews submissions, grades work.

S

Student

Sees only what's published to their class, submits before the deadline.

Entry 03 — the flow

From draft to grade, in order.

01

Draft

Teacher builds the assignment. Not visible to students yet.

02

Published

Visible to the assigned class. Students can submit.

03

Submitted

Late is fine — if the teacher allows it. Otherwise, the deadline holds.

04

Graded / Closed

Marks and feedback recorded. Closed stops new submissions, no exceptions.

Entry 04 — the rules don't bend

The system holds the line, so people don't have to.

RULEA teacher can't grade a class they don't teach.
RULEA closed assignment stops taking submissions — no exceptions, no back-channel.
RULEEvery edit to a submission is kept, not overwritten. Nothing quietly disappears.

Full submission history, every edit
